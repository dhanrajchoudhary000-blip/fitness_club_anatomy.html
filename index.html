EXERCISE DETAILS

Learn the movement, target muscles, and basic training guidance.

💪 Push-Ups

Target: Chest, shoulders, triceps

Beginner: 3 sets × 8–12 reps

Tip: Keep your body straight and control the movement.

🏋️ Squats

Target: Quads, glutes, hamstrings

Beginner: 3 sets × 10–15 reps

Tip: Keep your chest up and knees tracking over your feet.

🔥 Plank

Target: Core and abdominal muscles

Beginner: 3 sets × 20–30 seconds

Tip: Keep your hips level and breathe normally.

🏋️ Lat Pulldown

Target: Lats and upper back

Beginner: 3 sets × 8–12 reps

Tip: Pull smoothly toward your upper chest without swinging.

💪 Biceps Curl

Target: Biceps

Beginner: 3 sets × 10–12 reps

Tip: Keep your elbows close to your body.

🏋️ Shoulder Press

Target: Shoulders and triceps

Beginner: 3 sets × 8–12 reps

Tip: Use controlled movement and avoid excessive weight.

DAY 3 — JOINTS & MOVEMENT

Understand how joints move and why mobility matters.

🦴

Flexion

Flexion decreases the angle between two body segments. It happens when you bend a joint.

Example: Biceps Curl or Squat

↗️

Extension

Extension increases the angle between body segments, generally returning a joint toward a straighter position.

Example: Standing from a Squat

🔄

Rotation

Rotation occurs when a body part turns around its longitudinal axis.

Example: Controlled torso rotation

🧘

Mobility

Mobility is the ability to actively move a joint through a useful range of motion with control.

Practice: Gentle shoulder and hip circles

⚖️

Stability

Stability helps the body maintain controlled positions while producing or resisting movement.

Practice: Plank and controlled single-leg balance

🏃

Movement Patterns

Learn basic patterns such as squat, hinge, push, pull, carry and rotation.

Goal: Move with control before adding load.

DAY 5 — WARM-UP & PREPARATION

Prepare your body before training with controlled movement.

🔥

Why Warm Up?

A warm-up gradually prepares your body for exercise and helps you focus on movement quality.

🚶

Light Cardio

Start with 5–10 minutes of comfortable walking, cycling or another easy activity.

🔄

Dynamic Mobility

Use controlled movements such as arm circles, hip circles and bodyweight squats.

🏋️

Practice Sets

Before heavier resistance training, perform lighter practice sets to rehearse your technique.

💧

Hydration

Drink fluids regularly and pay attention to thirst, especially during longer or hotter workouts.

📝

Day 5 Challenge

Complete a 5–10 minute warm-up before your next workout and focus on smooth, controlled movements.
